# 38Games community conduct

We want project discussions to remain welcoming, useful and focused.

## Expected behavior

- Treat other people with respect.
- Give constructive feedback about the work rather than the person.
- Share enough context for others to understand your report or suggestion.
- Respect privacy, intellectual property and project confidentiality.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate disruption and the publication of another person's private information are not accepted.

Project maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary.
